Resetting destroys your message history

Your encryption keys are sealed with your password. Without it they cannot be recovered — not by us, not by anyone — so resetting replaces them. Every message you have already sent or received becomes permanently unreadable. If you still know your current password, sign in and change it from settings instead; that keeps your history.
