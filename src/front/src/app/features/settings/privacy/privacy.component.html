What others can see

Your name, username, bio and avatar are visible to anyone who can find you. Your avatar is stored unencrypted. Your email and phone number are not shown to other people.

Message content in private chats and groups is end-to-end encrypted; the server stores only ciphertext. To keep a copy of a conversation, use Export chat history on that chat's info screen.
