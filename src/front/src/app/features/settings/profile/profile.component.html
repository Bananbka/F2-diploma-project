Revoke a device you no longer have. Your encrypted chats re-key immediately, so nothing sent afterwards can be read with the keys it holds — but it keeps whatever it already received, which no server can take back.

Rotating your signed prekey gives forward secrecy to future messages: after one further rotation, someone who steals this device's keys can no longer use them to open messages sent under an older prekey. (The most recently retired prekey is kept for one extra rotation, so a message already in flight when you rotate still arrives.) It does not change your identity key or re-key any chat.
